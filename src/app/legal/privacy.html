Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Meetify is an independent project maintained by a single developer. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we protect it — in plain language.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email addressAccount creation and login
Password hash (bcrypt)Authentication — your plaintext password is never stored
UsernameDisplay name inside the app
Avatar imageOptional, only if you upload one
FeedbackThe text you send on the feedback page. It is anonymous and not linked to your account; an email is kept only if you add one to get a reply

We do not collect IP address logs, behavioral analytics, or advertising data.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to operate your account. We do not sell, share, rent, or otherwise disclose your data to any third party — ever.

We may occasionally email registered users about the service itself (updates, maintenance, security). These messages contain a one-click unsubscribe link; unsubscribing never affects account emails such as email verification and password reset.

Where data is stored

All data is stored on a server located in Switzerland. Switzerland operates under the Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP), which provides protections equivalent to or exceeding the EU GDPR.

Swiss-hosted · Strong data protection laws · No third-party data sharing

How long we keep your data

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all associated data — email, password hash, username, avatar — is permanently and irreversibly removed from our database.

Automatic error reporting

When a JavaScript error occurs in your browser, the app automatically sends a diagnostic report to our server. This report includes: the error message and stack trace, the URL of the page where it occurred, your browser name/version, OS platform, display language, viewport size, your user ID (if logged in), and an anonymous session ID generated for the current tab.

This data is used exclusively to diagnose and fix bugs. It is stored on our server, accessible only to administrators, and is never shared with any third party. No report is sent if no error occurs.

Cookies

We use a single session cookie (token) strictly required for authentication. No tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies are used.

Your rights

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correct — update inaccurate data via your profile settings
  • Delete — remove your account and all data via profile settings, or by emailing us

Email privacy@meetify.cc for any of these requests. We will respond within one month as required by applicable law.

Security

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (cost factor 10) — never stored in plaintext
  • All connections use HTTPS
  • Authentication uses short-lived JWT tokens in httpOnly cookies

Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the basis of contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): your email address and password are strictly necessary to create and operate your account. Without this data, the service cannot function.

We do not process data for any purpose beyond what is required to deliver the service.

Minimum age

You must be at least 16 years old to create an account. By registering, you confirm that you meet this requirement. If we become aware that a user is under 16, we will delete their account and all associated data without delay.

Data breach notification

In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours and inform affected users without undue delay.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@meetify.cc
