How privacy works

Two room modes, and an honest account of where your audio and video travel in each.

Peer-to-peer up to 8 people

Browsers connect to each other directly. Audio and video do not pass through our server. The server only helps browsers find each other to start the call.

SFU larger groups

For bigger rooms, media is forwarded by our media server to the other participants. It passes through the server to reach them and is not recorded or stored.

What we keep

  • Your account: email, username and a hashed password.
  • Room details: name, schedule, host and limits.
  • Support tickets you send us.

What we do not

  • No recordings of calls.
  • No analytics and no trackers.
  • No third-party scripts or fonts on this page.

The frontend is open for audit

Everything that runs in your browser is public: pages, scripts and styles. Read it, and check what it sends and where.

The server code is closed for security, because it handles accounts and sessions. Keeping the client public is how you verify what we say here.

Questions

Short answers. The privacy policy has the full version.

Read the privacy policy

Do I need an account?
Yes. You need an account to create a room or to join one.
Who can see my video and audio?
Only the people in the room. In peer-to-peer rooms the media goes directly between browsers. In SFU rooms it passes through our media server to reach the other participants.
Are calls recorded?
No. Meetify does not record calls.
How many people can join?
Peer-to-peer rooms hold up to 8 people. SFU rooms are for larger groups, and the host sets the limit when creating the room.
Why is the server code closed?
The server handles accounts, sessions and signaling, so we keep it closed for security. The frontend is public so you can check what runs in your browser.
Is it free?
Yes. Meetify is free for noncommercial use under the Polyform Noncommercial license.
